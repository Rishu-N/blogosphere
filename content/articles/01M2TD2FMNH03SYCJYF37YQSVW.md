# Starting Something New

Every blog starts the same way: an empty folder and a stubborn feeling that
something is worth writing down. This one is no different, except that the
site itself is going to keep changing shape around whatever I end up
writing.

## Why build it this way

I wanted a blog that behaved less like a filing cabinet and more like a
living thing — something that visibly remembers what's been read and leans
into it, the way a room feels different depending on who has been sitting
in it.

## What to expect

Expect a mix of technology notes, half-finished travel stories, and the
occasional attempt at cooking something I definitely should have researched
first. The color of this page will drift as those categories get read more
or less over time, so if you come back next month, it might not look quite
the same.

## One more thing

None of this is precious. If a post is short, its preview is just the post.
If it's long, you get the opening section and a link to keep going. That's
the whole idea.
